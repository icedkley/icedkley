## 🎯 What I Build

**High-converting landing pages for small blue-collar businesses** — plumbers, electricians, roofers, landscapers, HVAC, cleaners, and other local trades.

I focus on three things that actually move the needle for a local service business:

| Priority | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| ⚡ **Speed** | Lightweight pages, optimized images, minimal bloat — fast on a phone on a 4G connection, because that's where your customers are. |
| 🔍 **SEO** | Local search structure: clean headings, schema markup, location-aware copy, fast Core Web Vitals — so you show up when someone searches "plumber near me." |
| 📈 **Conversion** | One clear call-to-action above the fold, click-to-call buttons, trust signals (reviews, licenses, service areas), and forms that don't scare people off. |

**The goal is simple:** a visitor lands, understands what you do in 5 seconds, and calls or books.
